# Independent visual review

Blind predictions were sealed in reviewer.csv before opening any QA sheet. Only 48-blind.jpg was used for the blind classification. No answer key, generation records, source layers or other reports were opened.

48/48 predictions: 24 kit, 24 whole. These are visual guesses, not measured accuracy. Repeated architectural silhouettes and repeated facade families strongly influenced classification; this is not a clean test of whether a single unseen assembled house can be distinguished from a painted house. Confidence is subjective.

All six QA sheets were viewed and all C001-C216 inspected: 72 minor awkward results (36 projecting-canopy contact/join concerns; 36 upper-front panel integration concerns), 144 noissue results, zero obvious major failure at this viewing scale. Repeated wording records the same visible concern recurring across the full combination family, not sampling. After sealing the blind result, blind/16.png and blind/39.png were additionally viewed to understand the recurring canopy and upper-panel appearance; blind predictions were not changed.

Limitations: contact sheets and two supporting transparent PNGs only; no pixel-level alpha inspection, engine placement, animation, collision, camera/zoom sweep or runtime validation. Minor findings are aesthetic judgments at sheet scale, not proof of invalid geometry. A noissue row means no visible issue found at this scale, not an assertion of perfect full-resolution integration. Mixed roof materials alone were not treated as defects.
