The 12 open-ended answers in `final-blind-responses.csv` were sealed before viewing either the 32-variant small-scale proof or the city-context proof. Only `proofs/02-blind-trades.jpg` and `blind/01.png` through `blind/12.png` were inspected for recognition. No occupation list, answer key, brief, generation records, or other review was read.

Confidence is subjective recognition confidence, not an accuracy score. Slots 06, 07, and 12 are tentative. Slot 03 identifies a textile trade but cannot reliably distinguish manufacture from retail. An accuracy count requires a separate comparison with the hidden key by the coordinating reviewer; this reviewer cannot substantiate 10/12.

## Proof review

After sealing, inspected `proofs/03-zoom-06.jpg` and `proofs/01-city-context.jpg` only. No answers were revised.

### Small-scale 32-variant proof

- The repeated house remains visually dominant. Props are consistently smaller than the door-to-roof height, with no oversized prop dominating a panel. The clear house-to-prop gaps establish freestanding silhouettes.
- Bread, fish, the anvil, planks, cloth frame, pale fleece, and the red sign remain more readily distinguishable than the thin pole or small cargo groups. Slots 06, 14, 18, 20, 28, 30, 31, and especially 32 lose much of their semantic detail at the displayed scale. Slot 07 still does not reliably reveal its cargo to this reviewer.
- The lower-detail variants read as furnishing or activity accents; they should not be represented as independently legible occupation labels at this size. A viewer can see that an object is present without knowing its exact type.
- Sparse placement avoids clutter in this isolated sheet. It does not test several props clustered around one house or packed along a street.
- Warm wood, ochre, pale cloth and restrained red fit the house palette. Upper surfaces generally carry highlights and supports are darker. No conspicuous reversed lighting or strong halo is visible here; the tiny rendering limits scrutiny of individual cast shadows and edges.
- No prop intersects the house wall in this sheet. This visual observation does not verify alpha geometry, footprints, pivots, or runtime collision.

### City context

- The two visibly added examples are a bread display beside a small red-roofed house and a red freestanding sign beside a much larger building. The explicitly labelled offline crops enlarge these examples. This is compositing/context evidence, not proof of live installation or gameplay.
- Bread is recognizable in the main view and clearly legible in crop A. Its brightness and definition are slightly stronger than the surrounding soft ground but remain compatible with the warm scene. The thin supports are visible without a conspicuous large platform.
- The sign gives a useful red accent and retains its emblem in the crop; the precise animal is less certain in the full scene. Its right side is close to the large building edge, so the visual separation is less generous than the isolated sheet. I do not observe a definite wall-pixel intersection at the available resolution. Close adjacency must not be reported as proven wall overlap.
- The bread tray has visible ground separation from the adjacent house. No definite bread-to-wall overlap is visible. Neither example visibly covers a doorway.
- Both props are modest in relation to nearby people and entrances. Two placements do not establish scale consistency for all 32 variants. The surrounding city is already dense; these two accents do not visibly overwhelm it, but multiple props per facade remain untested.
- Lighting is broadly compatible with warm illuminated roofs and pale upper surfaces. The proof does not establish exact light-vector or shadow-footprint agreement.

## Limits and disposition

Recognition answers are sealed and unscored. No hidden-key accuracy threshold is claimed. Main weaknesses are cargo ambiguity in slot 07, tentative pole interpretation in slot 06, generic trading interpretation in slot 12, and semantic loss for the smallest variants. No definite actual wall overlap was observed in the supplied three proofs. This is limited visual QA of static images, not runtime rendering, installation, occlusion, placement-order, navigation, or performance verification.
